About Us

Welcome to Central Institute of Business & Technology, Benin

An Innovation Enterprise Institution (IEI) of the Federal Ministry of Education. Accredited by the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE) and Joint Admission and Matriculation Board (JAMB) to award National Diploma (NID).It gives me great pleasure to welcome you to Central Institute of Business & Technology Benin, where we heartily believe that “Education is man’s most important enterprise”. However, a gap separates those who seek to expand their knowledge, from those concerned with the nature of the learning process itself.

Working for a higher education qualification offers you a unique opportunity to develop a depth of knowledge and specialised skills, which will be of benefit in the future. All our courses are designed to ensure that they are of the highest quality and of direct relevance to industry, commerce, and the professions; indeed we welcome the practical involvement of people from all of these area’s in the design and delivery of all our courses.

Central Institute of Business & Technology is a new Polytechnic that will be dedicated to conveying the value of analytical and critical thought, preparing you for a variety of careers in an ever changing business and creative world as an innovative professional with something to offer.
